# Interview decisions and safe defaults

This proposal was built non-interactively (no blocking questions were asked before creating the branch, per instruction) using the safest defaults below. Every assumption is recorded here rather than silently baked into the design.

## Questions that would materially change the proposal, and the default taken

| # | Question | Safe default applied | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| H2-001 | Should a future first release be staff-only, or may it show carefully reviewed patient education? | **Staff-only** for the first release; patient education is Phase 6, its own separately gated review. | open -- needs product confirmation |
| H2-002 | What launch jurisdictions must be covered (Israel, China/Hong Kong, Australia, India, UK, US, other)? | None assumed. TCM-practitioner credentialing and PSK-style jurisdiction-specific clinician pathways are recorded as needing per-jurisdiction definition before Phase 6. | open -- needs product decision |
| H2-003 | Is the first scope adults only? | **Yes, adults only.** Pregnancy, lactation, pediatrics, stem-cell transplantation, CAR-T therapy, severe organ impairment, perioperative care, and intensive immunosuppression are explicitly noted as requiring separate specialist review and do not inherit adult-general content anywhere in this proposal. | default, adults-only with named exclusions |
| H2-004 | Who will fill the oncology/pharmacy, evidence-methodology, Chinese-medicine, and oncology-dietitian review roles? | Unfilled; recorded as **TBD** in every approval record's schema. No card or template can pass the publish gate until named. | open -- needs product decision |
| H2-005 | Have the Ayurveda and homeopathy proposals been merged, or should this remain independently reviewable? | **Confirmed not merged** (`origin/main` has no Ayurveda or homeopathy content as of this branch's creation; both sibling branches are separate open PRs). This proposal branches independently from `origin/main` and does not depend on, cherry-pick, or reference specific unmerged commits from either sibling branch. | resolved for this branch; re-check before Phase 2 in case a sibling merges first |
| H2-006 | Should the adjacent alternative-modality screen remain staff-only, with only approved Chinese-herbal records eligible for future patient display? | **Yes.** `alternative_modality_screening.json` is a discovery/audit artifact only; no screen_decision, by itself, authorizes any patient-visible content, and the integration plan scopes the screening-record table as staff-only. | default |
| H2-007 | Are Chinese-language databases and a qualified bilingual clinical reviewer available? | **No**, not confirmed available in this pass. ChiCTR, SinoMed, CNKI, and Wanfang were not searched; no bilingual reviewer was available. No claim of a comprehensive Chinese-language search is made anywhere in this proposal. | open -- needs infrastructure/staffing decision before Phase 4 for any card resting on Chinese-language primary literature |

## Additional open items surfaced during source review

| # | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| H2-008 | YIV-906/PHY906's current official trial registrations and posted results were **not** independently re-verified in this pass; `card-yiv906-phy906-investigational` is deliberately left without a specific certainty claim. | Full registry verification was out of scope for a single non-interactive pass; recorded honestly as pending rather than asserting a verified-sounding status. | open -- needs registry verification before Phase 4 |
| H2-009 | The five injectable proprietary Chinese medicines (Aidi, Shenqi Fuzheng, Kanglaite, Compound Kushen, Brucea javanica oil) are registered as one category-level record, not five individual evidence cards. | Individual composition, jurisdiction, trial-quality, and pharmacovigilance review for each product was out of scope for this pass; pooling them into a single record with an honest "not evaluated" status was judged safer than fabricating five under-verified individual cards. | open -- each product needs its own dedicated Phase 4 review before any product-specific card is created |
| H2-010 | Animal/mineral-containing patent medicines (HuaChanSu, Pien Tze Huang) are explicitly kept `out_of_scope_adjacent` pending dedicated toxicology and identity review, rather than modeled inside the herb evidence framework. | These contain non-botanical ingredients (toad venom, musk, mineral/animal-derived gallstone material) with their own distinct toxicity and identity concerns that the herb-focused framework in this proposal was not designed to evaluate. | open -- needs a dedicated review track, likely outside this proposal's scope entirely |
| H2-011 | The HealthTree source's exact content, authorship, and publication date remain `access_unverified`. | Access verification was not completed in this pass; content was not inferred or substituted with a similarly titled document. | open -- re-attempt access verification in a future pass |
| H2-012 | Reviewer identity for the pull request: "Jonathan Zouari" is named as the confirming reviewer. This repository is a fork of `JonathanZouari/herbal-evidence` on GitHub (confirmed via repository metadata in the sibling proposals' review process), so the identity is real, but he is not a collaborator on this fork. | Consistent with how the sibling Ayurveda and homeopathy proposals' PRs were handled -- avoid guessing a handle; use a comment-mention or open the PR against the fork parent directly, per the repository owner's prior direction for this repository. | resolved -- follow whichever routing the repository owner most recently specified for this repository |
| H2-013 | No nutrition template is cancer-type-specific, and no new cancer-specific diet was added even where source material (e.g. PMC5073821's cancer-type-specific respondent groups) might suggest one. | No current oncology guideline was identified supporting a distinct diet for any exact disease/treatment context in this pass; inventing one from survey data would violate the evidence-vs-tradition separation this proposal exists to enforce. | resolved -- by design, enforced by `DietTemplateTests.test_no_diet_template_has_a_cancer_context` |
