# Clinical evidence map

Machine-readable form: `knowledge/homeopathy-oncology/example_evidence_cards.json` (13 cards). Prose summary, in the same order as the proposal brief's "initial cancer-context evidence map," plus one additional card surfaced by the local-handout audit.

1. **Any cancer, homeopathy generally** (`blocked_unsupported`) -- reliable evidence does not support a cancer-treatment or prevention claim for homeopathy as a general modality; never substitute or delay oncology care.
2. **NSCLC, individualized add-on homeopathy, quality of life and survival** (`blocked_integrity`) -- the prominent 2020 Frass et al. paper is retracted for data falsification; cannot support a claim or recommendation regardless of its original reported effect size.
3. **Advanced breast cancer, Banerji protocol, feasibility** (`blocked_no_results`) -- NCT02190539 is withdrawn with no enrollment and no results.
4. **Metastatic breast cancer, homeopathy replacing standard care** (`avoid`) -- a 2025 case report is a treatment-substitution harm context; emphasize timely oncology/palliative-care re-engagement without blaming the patient.
5. **Cancer patients during treatment, wellbeing/fatigue/QOL/anxiety/depression** (`insufficient_evidence`) -- heterogeneous, generally low-quality studies; the 2022 systematic review found no conclusive evidence beyond placebo; the most-cited positive trial has disclosed responder-only analysis bias and an investigator-concentration concern.
6. **Breast cancer during chemotherapy, Cocculine, CINV** (`insufficient_evidence`) -- multicenter RCT null result; never replace prescribed antiemetics.
7. **Breast-cancer survivors on endocrine therapy, hot flushes** (`insufficient_evidence`) -- mixed positive/null findings across fixed-combination and individualized preparations that are not interchangeable; one cited combination product contains amyl nitrate, a pharmacologically active vasodilator.
8. **Treatment-associated oral mucositis, Traumeel S** (`research_only`) -- small early positive pilot; later/larger replication status pending full re-verification; never generalized to classical homeopathy.
9. **Breast radiotherapy, topical Calendula, radiodermatitis** (`research_only`) -- older, small preliminary positive signal for a topical botanical preparation specifically; not evidence for ultra-dilute homeopathy generally.
10. **Breast surgery, Arnica, bleeding/seroma/pain/recovery** (`insufficient_evidence`) -- mixed results across mother-tincture and high-dilution preparations, which are not the same intervention.
11. **Chemically induced liver tumors (rats) and sarcomas (mice)** (`research_only`) -- animal-model-only; human inference prohibited by design.
12. **Prostate and breast cancer cell lines, multiple preparations** (`research_only`) -- the handout's positive in-vitro citations are preserved alongside an independently identified negative in-vitro study (Thangapazham et al.) on materially overlapping preparations; no clinical inference either direction.
13. **Gallbladder/liver/lung/oral/pancreatic/stomach cancers, Psorinum-6x as reported sole treatment** (`avoid`) -- an uncontrolled, single-center, retrospective 246-patient series including a "sole anti-cancer treatment... complete response... without adverse effects" claim for metastatic gallbladder adenocarcinoma; this is the treatment-substitution pattern this proposal exists to flag, not evidence of safety or efficacy. Surfaced by this pass's own audit of the local handout rather than the brief's initial list -- included because "at least" the listed examples were required, and this is one of the highest-stakes claims found.

## What every card answers

Per card: exact population/cancer/stage/treatment context; whether the claim is cancer control or a supportive-care symptom; exact product, ingredients, potency (as study metadata), preparation, route, and co-interventions; comparator and sample size; prespecified outcomes and whether objective or self-reported; result direction (positive/null/negative/mixed/unavailable/retracted/case-series-uncontrolled); risk-of-bias, multiplicity, attrition, confounding, and generalizability concerns; publication integrity status; what was **not** shown; safety/interaction/toxicity/care-delay risks; and what a patient should do instead (continue standard care, use proven supportive care, disclose products to the oncology team). `scripts/validate_homeopathy_proposal.py` enforces the presence of every one of these fields on every card.
